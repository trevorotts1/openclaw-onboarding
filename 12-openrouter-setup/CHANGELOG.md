## [7.0.3] - 2026-10-13

### Changed
- Model doctrine cleanup: removed named-model prescriptions from INSTALL.md, INSTRUCTIONS.md, EXAMPLES.md, QC.md, CORE_UPDATES.md, and openrouter-setup-full.md. Model selection now follows the client-choice pattern: the client selects their model, or their configured default model is used, and the system may recommend a capability profile with its reason stated, as guidance the client can override.
