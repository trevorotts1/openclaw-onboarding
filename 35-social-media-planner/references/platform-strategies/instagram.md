## Creative guidance (organic)

Original brand-relevant creative; match the idea to a carousel, image or Reel; opening visually clear, captions legible, vertical safe areas intentional. Watch time #1; DM shares and saves critical. Consult the account's professional-dashboard recommendations.

## Advertising lane (kept separate)

Separate lane: paid placement follows Meta ads guidance; organic learnings must not be presented as paid results.
