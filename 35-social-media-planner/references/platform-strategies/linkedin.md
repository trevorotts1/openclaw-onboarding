## Creative guidance (organic)

Lead with professional relevance and a concrete insight or evidence; custom visuals, native video or document storytelling when the delivery path supports it; invite useful discussion. Never copy Instagram captions unchanged.

## Advertising lane (kept separate)

Separate lane: sponsored content follows LinkedIn Ads guidance; organic engagement is not ad performance.
