## Style / Definition
The Transactional objective delivers what a buyer already paid for: access, the receipt, the first step, and (in the event variant) the logistics of the event they bought. It persuades nothing. Every transactional email answers one operational question the buyer has right now.

## Purpose & Intention
After payment the buyer's only question is "how do I get in, and what happens next?" A transactional email answers it plainly and stops. It is the Signature Launch `welcome_access` family's objective, and the prover accepts it there; the interactive one-block questionnaire never offers it.

## How To Write It
1. **Access first.** The access link (or the support address when the brief has no link) sits in the top third of the first email.
2. **Facts only from the order record and the approved brief.** Amount charged, plan, order reference, session dates and room links are never invented.
3. **No offer, no price pitch, no urgency.** Non-selling event nurture (preparation, what to expect, logistics, reminders) stays non-selling: no upsell, no countdown, no scarcity.
4. **Opt-out rules differ by email.** The receipt and access email is transactional and is not suppressed by a marketing opt-out; event nurture emails honor it.
5. **Founder's actual name** in every close.

## Use Cases
- The Signature Launch welcome / access email (payment succeeded)
- The 10-email welcome variant: one transactional welcome plus nine non-selling event nurture emails
