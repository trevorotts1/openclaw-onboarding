## Method — how to generate the 30 program titles

Follow this sequence. Do not skip steps; do not merge steps.

1. **Ground in the dossier.** Read the avatar dossier and the blended tone document.
   Write down the avatar's primary goal and the one identity shift they are buying —
   the "from X to Y" the program delivers.

2. **Mine the book's subject.** From the intake's book subject and the niche,
   extract the concrete transformation, the audience, and the unfair advantage the
   program offers.

3. **Generate in five banks of six.** Produce 30 candidates across five
   complementary angles (six per bank) so the list has range:
   - Method names — name the step-by-step system (e.g. "The Quiet Authority System").
   - Outcome names — name the end state (e.g. "The Team That Promotes Itself").
   - Metaphor names — a concrete image that carries the promise (e.g. "Trade
     Control for Trust").
   - Contrast names — the "from/to" made punchy (e.g. "From Star to Steward").
   - Provocative names — a claim the avatar instantly wants (e.g. "Ship Without You").

4. **Distinctness pass.** Remove near-duplicates and any title that could be
   confused with another on the list. Every title must survive on its own.

5. **Count exactly 30.** The final document must contain EXACTLY 30 numbered items.
   The machine counts numbered lines on the stripped text and hard-fails on any
   number other than 30 (AF-BK-433-COUNTS). Verify your list is 1. through 30. and
   contains no bulleted sub-lines.

6. **Quality bar.** Each title: 1-8 words, concrete not vague, benefit-forward,
   free of hollow hype words ("ultimate", "amazing"), and specific enough that the
   ideal avatar would click. Prefer titles that could serve as the flagship program
   name; the strongest candidate becomes the ProductName used in the deck data.

Output only the numbered markdown list.
