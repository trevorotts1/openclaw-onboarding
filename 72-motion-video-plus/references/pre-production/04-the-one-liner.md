# Pre-production 04: The one-liner

Before the brief, before the scenes, write one sentence that the whole video must earn.

## The exercise

State what the video is in a single sentence of under 20 words, naming the viewer, the change, and the feeling. Examples of the shape (not copy):

- "A skeptical founder watches her churn problem become a solved dashboard in 90 seconds and feels relief."
- "A busy parent sees dinner planning collapse into one tap and feels ten minutes handed back."

If you cannot say it in one sentence, the concept is not ready. Rewrite until it fits.

## Why it matters

Every later decision is a test against this sentence: does this scene, this line, this transition serve the one-liner? Anything that does not is cut. The one-liner goes at the top of the director's brief, and the critic reads it before scoring.
