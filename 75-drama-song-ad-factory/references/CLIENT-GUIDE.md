# Video Dramatization Client Guide

Everything you need to know before you start: what we make, who makes it, every question we ask, and how to be ready.

## Page 1 - Turn your offer into a video people feel

**Headline:** Your offer, told as a story with an original song.

- You answer a few quick questions. We handle the rest.
- You get a video dramatization with music, starring your characters, with captions and your call to action.
- You approve the script, pick 1 of 3 song versions, and sign off on the storyboard before any video is made.
- 3-, 5- and 10-minute videos also come with 60- and 90-second clips for social media.

> **Top-up reminder:** If your video uses KIE.ai or OpenRouter, top up your credits there first so your video does not stop halfway.

**Image idea:** A phone screen playing a glowing, cinematic scene with a song waveform running beneath it and a "Your Offer" ticket in the corner.

## Page 15 - Setup question: your AI models

**Headline:** Who builds it, and who checks it?

- **We ask:** "Which AI should build your video, and which should check the work? OpenRouter is recommended because it's faster; Ollama works too."
  1. Recommended setup - an OpenRouter model builds, Claude Sonnet checks (recommended)
  2. Choose my own - reply with the build model and the check model, e.g. "DeepSeek builds, Sonnet checks"

  - A different model for checking than the one that builds.
- **Skipped when:** You say "use my defaults" and your setup already has a build and check pair.

## Page 30 - What happens after you answer

**Headline:** We take it from here.

1. You reply "yes" to the recap.
2. We write the story and script with the lyrics woven in. You approve it.
3. We create your characters and compose your song. You pick your favorite of 3 versions.
4. We build the storyboard with a picture per shot. You sign off.
5. We film the video shots and lip-sync your characters.
6. We edit it all together with captions and your call to action, and run quality checks.
7. We deliver your final video. 3-, 5- and 10-minute videos also come with 60- and 90-second clips for social media.

**You bring the story. Our Hollywood team brings it to life.**
