# Drama Song Video - Client Guide

This guide shows you every question we ask, how to answer it well, and how to get ready.
You can read it before you start, or keep it open while you answer.

---

## 1. The opening

> Turn your offer into a music video people actually feel. Answer a few quick questions, and we handle the rest: write the story and script with the song lyrics woven in; create your characters (or bring back ones you've saved); compose an original song in the music style you choose; create every image and build the storyboard, shot by shot; make the video shots and lip-sync the singers; edit it all together with captions and your call to action. You approve the script, pick your favorite of 3 song versions, and sign off on the storyboard before any video is made. Longer videos also come with 60- and 90-second clips for social media.
>
> **Before we start:** if your video uses KIE.ai or OpenRouter models, top up your credits there first so your video doesn't stop halfway.
>
> Let's start - just a few quick questions.

---

## 2. Before you start (checklist)

- [ ] Top up your KIE.ai credits, if your video uses KIE.ai models.
- [ ] Top up your OpenRouter credits, if your video uses OpenRouter models.
- [ ] Decide which AI model **builds** your video (for example DeepSeek or an Ollama model).
- [ ] Decide which AI model **checks** the work (for example the Sonnet chain). Pick a different one from the builder.
- [ ] Have your offer name and its link ready.
- [ ] Write one line: who the video is for.
- [ ] Write one line: what viewers should do next.
- [ ] Gather your logo, product photos and any proof (a testimonial or a result).
- [ ] Decide the most you want to spend.
- [ ] If you have saved characters, know their names.
- [ ] Set aside 20 to 30 minutes for the three approvals.

---

## 3. The questions

Each question shows: what we ask, what it decides, two good answers, how to prepare, and when it is skipped.

### Setup question: your AI models

**We ask:** "Which AI model builds your video, and which one checks the work? Example: DeepSeek builds, the Sonnet chain checks."

**What it decides:** Who creates your video, and who double-checks it before you see it.

**Good answers**
- "DeepSeek builds, the Sonnet chain checks."
- "An Ollama model builds, the Sonnet chain checks."

**Prepare**
- Check which model accounts you have.
- Make sure each account has credit (KIE.ai, OpenRouter).
- Pick a different model to check than the one that builds.

**Skipped when:** You say "use my defaults" and your setup already has a build and check pair.

### 1. Your offer

**We ask:** "What product or offer are we promoting, and what link or assets should we use?"

**What it decides:** What the story, the song and the call to action are about.

**Good answers**
- "My 6-week Confident Speaker program, https://example.com/speaker. Use my logo and the testimonial PDF."
- "Our Sunday family meal box. The link is on our site, and I'll send 5 food photos."

**Prepare**
- Write the offer name.
- Copy the exact web address.
- Gather your logo and photos.
- Have one proof point ready.

**Skipped when:** Never. We only follow up if the link is missing or the spelling looks wrong.

### 2. Your audience and next step

**We ask:** "Who is this video for, and what should they do after watching it? e.g. 'Women 35-55 who want a second income - register for my free masterclass.'"

**What it decides:** Who the story speaks to, and what the ending asks them to do.

**Good answers**
- "Women 35-55 who want a second income. Register for my free masterclass."
- "First-time buyers in Atlanta. Book a 15-minute call."

**Prepare**
- Describe your best client in one sentence.
- Pick one action: register, book, call or buy.
- Know where that action happens (link, phone or booking page).

**Skipped when:** Partly. If you already gave one half, we only ask for the missing half.

### 3. Your character

**We ask:** "Do you want to create a new character for this video, or use one you've used before? You have N characters saved with us. 1 Create new (recommended). 2+ Use a saved character by name."

**What it decides:** Who stars in your video.

**Good answers**
- "1. A warm 40-year-old braider named Tasha with locs."
- "2. Use Marcus, my saved agent."

**Prepare**
- For a new character, write the name, age, look and clothing.
- Have a reference photo if you want a likeness.
- Check your saved list.

**Skipped when:** You accept the recommended choice (create new).

### 4. Length

**We ask:** "How long should the video be?" Options: 60 seconds (recommended), 90 seconds, 3 minutes plus 60- and 90-second clips, 5 minutes plus clips, 10 minutes plus clips.

**What it decides:** The size of the story, the song and the price.

**Good answers**
- "60 seconds. It's for Instagram."
- "3 minutes plus clips. It's for my webinar page and social media."

**Prepare**
- Decide where the video will play.
- Remember that longer videos cost more.
- Decide whether you want social clips too.

**Skipped when:** You accept 60 seconds.

### 5. Music style

**We ask:** "Which music style?" Options: Soul Ballad (slow and heartfelt, big emotional chorus, recommended), R&B Flow (rap verses on the beat, smooth sung hook), Soul Rise (starts slow and sad, lifts into an upbeat hopeful groove).

**What it decides:** The sound and feeling of your song.

**Good answers**
- "Soul Rise. It starts with the problem and ends hopeful."
- "R&B Flow. Rap verses about owning your first home."

**Prepare**
- Decide the feeling you want viewers to have.
- Note one or two songs you like.
- Match the style to your audience.

**Skipped when:** You accept Soul Ballad.

### 7. Video model

**We ask:** "Which video model?" Options: MiniMax H3 (recommended), Seedance 2.5, Seedance 2.0 Mini, Google Veo 3.1. Your card shows the price for your length.

**What it decides:** The quality and the cost of the video shots.

**Good answers**
- "MiniMax H3. Good quality at a fair price."
- "Google Veo 3.1. This is my big launch video."

**Prepare**
- Read the price on your card.
- Decide how much quality is worth for this video.
- Make sure the matching account has credit.

**Skipped when:** You accept MiniMax H3.

### 8. Budget

**We ask:** "What's the most you want to spend on this video?" 1 = the price on your card, including a 20% redo allowance (recommended). 2 = a different maximum.

**What it decides:** The spending limit we stay under.

**Good answers**
- "1. The recommended price."
- "2. A maximum of $150."

**Prepare**
- Pick a firm ceiling.
- Remember the redo allowance covers fixes.
- Check your KIE.ai and OpenRouter balances against it.

**Skipped when:** You accept option 1.

### 9. Storyboard approval

**We ask:** You get a written card and a still image for each shot. "Approve, or tell us what to change."

**What it decides:** What every shot will look like before any video is made.

**Good answers**
- "Approved. Change shot 4 to show the storefront."
- "Approved as is."

**Prepare**
- Set aside 10 minutes.
- Check the product, logo and characters in every shot.
- Keep your call to action in mind.

**Skipped when:** Never. It is required.

### 10. Song approval

**We ask:** You get 3 labelled versions. "Which one do you want?"

**What it decides:** The song that carries your whole video.

**Good answers**
- "Version B. I can't stop humming the chorus."
- "Version A, but make the second verse shorter."

**Prepare**
- Listen to all three, on speakers or headphones.
- Pick for your audience, not only your own taste.
- Check that your offer name is sung clearly and spelled right.

**Skipped when:** Never. It is required.

### 11. Script approval

**We ask:** You get the story and lyrics before the song is made. "Approve, or tell us what to change."

**What it decides:** The words your audience will hear.

**Good answers**
- "Approved. Change 'free class' to 'free masterclass'."
- "Approved as is."

**Prepare**
- Read the lyrics out loud.
- Check the offer name, link and call to action.
- Flag anything you would not say to a client.

**Skipped when:** Never. It is required.

### The recap

**We ask:** "Reply 'yes' to start, or the number of a line to change it."

**Good answers:** "Yes." or "Change 4 to 90 seconds."

**Prepare:** Re-read it once and check the budget line.

**Skipped when:** Never. Nothing starts until you reply.

---

## 4. The three approvals

You stay in control. Nothing moves forward until you say so.

1. **Script.** You read the story and lyrics. Changes are easiest here.
2. **Song.** You hear 3 versions and pick your favorite.
3. **Storyboard.** You see a card and a still image for each shot, and sign off before any video is made.

---

## 5. What happens after you answer

1. You reply "yes" to the recap.
2. We write the story and script with the lyrics woven in. You approve it.
3. We compose your song. You pick your favorite of 3 versions.
4. We create your characters and every image, and build the storyboard. You sign off.
5. We make the video shots and lip-sync the singers.
6. We edit it all together with captions and your call to action.
7. We deliver your video. Longer videos also come with 60- and 90-second clips for social media.
