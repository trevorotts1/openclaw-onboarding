Evening routines for restless sleepers

Chronic back pain keeps millions of adults awake at night, and most of them never mention it to a doctor.

This guide explains how a simple evening stretching routine can reduce morning stiffness within a few weeks, without any equipment and without a gym membership.

Readers who tried the routine for a month reported falling asleep faster and waking with less stiffness than before they started.
