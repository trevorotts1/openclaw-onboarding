Two recorded deviations from the bare argument list in the unit brief, both
deliberate and both inside the runner's own CLI:
  --once            one launchd fire == one scheduled tick, so each fire is a
                    separate launchd-spawned process. Without it the first
                    process holds the singleton lease for its whole life and
                    later launchd fires cannot tick.
  --tick-work-ms 45000
                    a 45s in-tick work window is what lets a real `kill -9`
                    land between the durable pending-action commit and its
                    consumption. At the default 0ms that window is
                    sub-millisecond, so an "interrupted tick" could not be
                    produced honestly.

kickstart, no CLI invocation of runner.mjs at any point.
