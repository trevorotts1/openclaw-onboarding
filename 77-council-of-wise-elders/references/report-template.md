# Council Report Template Reference

The verdict page has one fixed order. Surfaces may style it; they may
not reorder it, drop sections, or promote a bare score into the
verdict.

## Verdict page order

1. **Recommendation.** One recommendation, stated plainly, with its
   mode (full, quick, or consult) and its ratification state. A quick
   council shows `ratification: not_run_by_quick_mode_policy`.
2. **Conditions.** The conditions the recommendation depends on,
   including any coverage or critical-abstention limits from
   scoring.md, stated in those terms.
3. **Three main reasons.** The three strongest supporting reasons,
   each traceable to seat findings.
4. **Strongest concern.** The single strongest objection, attributed
   to its seat, unedited in substance.
5. **Immediate next actions.** The next useful steps, marked as
   proposals. Nothing here is a created task or an executed action.
6. **Full scorecard.** Every option, every seat score, abstentions
   with reasons, coverage, and the route identity statement from
   model-routing.md where applicable.
7. **Detailed rounds and sources.** Round summaries, cruxes and how
   they resolved, and the source appendix with evidence classes kept
   separate per source-policy.md.

## Standing copy

The report introduces itself with: "Big decisions deserve more than
one perspective." Every seat named in the report carries its exact
profile label at first mention.

## Redacted team reports

A report shared with a team is a separate artifact, generated from the
stored result, never an edit of the original. Redaction covers the
body, metadata, source appendix, embedded assets, and filenames.
Private-session content never enters a company-visible artifact.

## Samples and replay

Sample and replay reports are labeled as samples and replays in the
artifact itself, in its title and its metadata, and can never create
production actions.
