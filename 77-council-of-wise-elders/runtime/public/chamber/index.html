How it works

You ask one question and name the options. The advisors work through it in phases, and the council ends with one recommendation and the reasons behind it. Nothing is final until the last phase.

Three sizes: a light question uses 3 advisors and exactly 2 rounds, a medium question uses all nine in up to 4 rounds, and a heavy question uses all nine in up to 10 rounds.

  1. Brief. The chair reads your question and freezes it with your options, so every advisor answers the same thing.
  2. Openings. Each advisor writes an independent first position, without seeing what the others wrote.
  3. Fault lines. The chair finds the points where the advisors really disagree.
  4. Debate. The advisors answer those disagreements and may change their minds, for as many rounds as the size allows.
  5. Vote. On the three older formats the chair drafts a resolution, and each advisor supports it, supports it with conditions, opposes it or abstains. Light, medium and heavy go straight from their last round to the verdict.
  6. Verdict. The chair checks the result, and the council gives one recommendation with its reasons.

A consult skips the fault lines, debate and vote. Each advisor is an AI perspective inspired by the published work of the named author. Not the individual.
