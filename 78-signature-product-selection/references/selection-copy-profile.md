# Skill 78 copy profile - specificity-led comparison (authored draft, S78-03)

Source authority: packet doc 08 sections 4-5 and doc 16 section 3. This is a
new design contract; the owner supplied no pre-existing exact selection-page
copy framework, and the Elevation example is a flow reference only, never a
verified visual template. Do not present this profile as established
BlackCEO source IP.

## Page shape

1. Bridging headline that acknowledges the visitor's interest (they arrived
   from the landing page's interest popup; do not re-sell the whole offer).
2. One plain paragraph: what choosing means and how the options differ.
3. One block per approved offer, in approved order, each with:
   exact offer name; best-fit buyer; primary outcome; differentiating
   inclusions stated specifically; important exclusions, visible; price and
   terms exactly as approved; a selection CTA reading "Choose [package]"
   or equally clear benefit-led wording.
4. Comparison matrix (owned component) when it improves clarity.
5. Common questions and trust information supported by actual evidence only.

## Specificity rule

Emotion and specificity stay together. Every benefit names the concrete
service behind it. Pattern (example only, not client copy):
"Get personal feedback - includes two private review sessions."
Banned as standalone copy: "VIP support", "premium access", "and more",
unless the time, channel, scope, or contents are defined in the same block.

## Claim discipline (enforced by validate_selection_content.py)

- No "Most popular", "Best seller", "Best value", savings percentages,
  invented discounts, or scarcity counts without an evidence reference.
- A recommendation names one option only with an explicit audience-tied
  reason, and only publishes when approved; unapproved recommendations
  appear in client review only.
- No urgency widgets, no giant guarantee banners unsupported by policy,
  no copied testimonials, no fabricated proof.
- Prices in copy must match an approved catalog price exactly; installment
  copy shows the due-now amount and the full total with its schedule.
- No price policy is inherited from the landing page (which carries no
  offer pricing); this page is where exact cost is explained.

## Voice

Preserve the founder's voice and the launch-level visual direction from the
brand file. Selection and checkout prioritize comprehension over expression
(doc 16 section 5).
