The Purple Billboard Playbook runs on a strategy called Hook

BEFORE THE EXAMPLES

The Month These Examples
Live In

Every example in this book was built for a monthly rhythm: 30 days
of content prepared for one monthly client approval. The month is
built and approved as one batch before it begins, and one billboard
still posts each day. Nothing is prepared week by week.

The caps, on every example
Teaching comments match the number promised in the post. No
example carries more than 8 authored comments in total. The
Bonus is unnumbered, always last, and carries at most 2 links.

Links and keywords stay put
A post link and a post keyword stay tied to the promise they were
first used for. They are never recycled for a different giveaway.
Each example in this book keeps its own keyword and its own
gift, from the post to the last reminder.

BEFORE THE EXAMPLES · CONTINUED

Where each example continues
After the thread, every example continues in the business
Messenger conversation. The page after each example shows that
conversation in full: the keyword, delivery in the chat first, the one
time pass, and the shared reminders.

KEYWORD CHAPTER · THE FLOW, CONTINUED

The promise lands first
The promised item, chapter one of the book, is delivered inside
the Messenger chat first, before any form, any email ask, or any
mobile ask. Nothing the reader was promised waits behind
contact details. After delivery, the conversation stays helpful and
needs based: ask what they are working on, answer from the gift,
and point to the next useful step.

More value, then the one time pass
After the gift lands, offer the additional value: the chapter map for
the rest of the book. Readers who want ongoing access get a one
time access pass. Ask for the mobile number first, and text the
pass to it. The email address is asked once, inside that same text
message with the pass. Every other email ask stays in the original
Messenger thread. Consent to receive the delivery is separate
from consent to receive marketing, and a reader who declines
marketing still gets the promise in full.

If contact details are still missing
One shared reminder sequence covers every missing detail
together, at 2 hours, 8 hours, and 20 hours, and only while the
channel still permits messaging. It stops at once on a refusal, on
capture of the details, or after the final message goes
unanswered. Nothing in it invents scarcity, a deadline, or a closing
offer.

FOR REAL ESTATE AGENTS

For Real Estate Agents
Inside are five complete billboards for a realtor working Maryland
and Washington, D.C., one for each kind of client: buyers, sellers,
investors, families settling an estate, and families facing a trustee
sale. Every example is written out in full: the post, all seven
comments, and the replies that follow, so you can copy it, post it,
and make it yours.
The billboard
One colored-background post a day on your personal profile.
Purple is the color this system runs on: one color, every day,
never changing, so people know your post before they read a
word. The post carries a hook, a numbered promise of six
things, and a pointer down to the comments.

The seven comments
The moment the billboard goes up, seven comments land
under it, in order. Comments 1 to 6 are the reveal: one
numbered item per comment, each complete on its own,
together paying off everything the post promised. A pointer
aimed at empty comments is a broken promise, so the reveal
always lands first.

The Bonus
The Bonus is the final comment: always last, unnumbered, and
always the same shape: follow me, join the list for the free gift,
join the community. It is the only comment carrying links, and
both doors live inside your Convert and Flow account. Swap the
two bracketed placeholders for your real list and community
links once, then never change the wording again.

The thread gets worked
After the comments land, the conversation starts, and it gets
answered. OpenClaw Skill 38 is the conversation brain: it reads
each reply, decides what helps, and answers in the thread,
steering warm readers toward your business Messenger
conversation, your list, your community, and your calendar.
Convert and Flow handles the messaging, the records, the
workflows, and the appointments underneath it. The brain is
never the native Convert and Flow AI. In each example below,
watch the replies under the comments: that is the thread being
worked.
How to use each example: post the billboard at your best hour (the
examples show 9:00 in the morning), land the comments in the
minutes right after it, in order, with the Bonus last, then let the
thread be worked through the day. Copy each one exactly the first
time you run it. After that, make the words your own.

The Realtor

Fact 1 of 6: What a trustee sale is. Most Maryland home loans
are held under a deed of trust. When payments fall behind,
the lender can ask a trustee to sell the home so the loan gets
repaid. That sale is the trustee sale, held as a public auction.
Like · Reply · 9:01 AM
R

The Realtor

Fact 2 of 6: It runs through the court, not around it. In
Maryland the process is filed in court, required notices go out
along the way, and after any sale the court reviews it before
it becomes final. Nothing lawful happens overnight or by
surprise.

Deadlines in this process are real, and some letters that look
like junk mail are not. Put each notice in one folder, in date
order. That folder becomes your family's map.
