## Method — how to derive the 4 Transformational Outcomes

Follow this sequence. Do not skip steps; do not merge steps.

1. **Ground in the dossier.** Read the avatar dossier and the blended tone
   document. State the avatar's primary goal and the current pain (the "before").
2. **Name the arc.** The offer moves the avatar through four stages of change.
   These four stages become the four Transformational Outcomes. Work from the
   source 4x3x3 logic: the program is delivered as FOUR phases, each phase carries
   ONE Transformational Outcome, and each phase maps to three book chapters.
3. **Write the four outcomes as identity shifts.** Each outcome names the state
   the avatar has become at the end of that phase — a noun phrase or short clause
   that reads as a claim ("From Star Performer to Team Multiplier"; "Trust That
   Runs the Team When You're Not in the Room"). Not a to-do, not a topic, not a
   promise of activity — a result the avatar becomes.
4. **Order matters.** Outcomes 1 through 4 must read as a progression: the
   transformation builds from the first outcome to the fourth, and the fourth is
   the capstone of the whole offer.
5. **Distinctness pass.** No two outcomes may say the same thing in different
   words. Each must be independently recognizable and independently marketable.
6. **Count exactly 4.** The final document must contain EXACTLY 4 numbered items.
   The machine counts numbered lines on the stripped text and hard-fails on any
   number other than 4 (AF-BK-433-COUNTS). Verify your list is 1. through 4. and
   contains no bulleted sub-lines.

Output only the numbered markdown list.
