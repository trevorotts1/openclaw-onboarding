# SOP-COUNCIL-01: Council of Wise Elders Deliberation

**What this governs:** How a Council of Wise Elders session (Skill 77, 77-council-of-wise-elders) is convened, run, ratified, and converted into follow-through work, in any company that has the skill installed. This SOP is process governance. It is not an advisor, not a tenth seat, and never a source of council content.

**The rule:** A council result is advice, never execution permission. No session runs without a shown brief, a client-selected route, and a purpose-bound run grant. No session exceeds 50 provider requests. Every seat output carries its exact profile label: "AI perspective inspired by the published work of [name]. Not the individual and not an endorsement."

**The process:**

1. **Convene.** Recognize the front-door phrases ("Ask my Council of Wise Elders", "Take this decision to the council", "Review this plan with the council", or a named-seat ask). Resolve existing sessions first: a stop, pause, resume, or "What did they decide?" acts on the existing session and never creates new work.
2. **Brief.** Build the decision brief from permitted context only. Mark unknowns as unknowns. Keep client-confirmed facts separate from any standard-company or scaffold placeholder data. Show the brief before any provider spend.
3. **Authorize.** Confirm the route (the client's selected model, or their configured default; recommendations are guidance only) and resolve the purpose-bound run grant. Never self-approve.
4. **Deliberate.** Run the bounded protocol in the skill's references/protocol.md: isolated openings, evidence-based challenge rounds resolving at most three cruxes (fact, numbers, values, execution), no fourth round, bounded ratification. The T. D. Jakes seat contributes three lenses inside one seat, never three votes. The chair runs process only and casts no advisory vote.
5. **Decide.** Compose the verdict in the fixed order of the skill's references/report-template.md: recommendation, conditions, three main reasons, strongest concern, immediate next actions, full scorecard, then detailed rounds and sources. Validate quotations after composition and before release. A GO-style label requires at least 80 percent weighted coverage and no unresolved critical-domain abstention.
6. **Convert, separately.** Follow-through is its own chain, each link separately authorized: preview the action plan, approve it, create tasks (exactly one parent task per company session, through the existing task core), then authorize execution on its own decision. A favorable vote creates nothing by itself.
7. **Review.** Record outcome reviews against the session when results are known, so the council's advice is judged by what actually happened. Scheduled reviews are deduplicated by decision and review period; the council never retriggers itself.
8. **Protect.** Private sessions stay visible only to their owner. Team copies are separate redacted artifacts (redaction covers body, metadata, appendix, embedded assets, and filenames). Samples and replays are labeled as such and never create production actions. Council state lives in the authoritative store for the installation's mode, never inside the skill folder.

**Anti-recursion:** A council never convenes a council about itself, never escalates a department-local question to exist, and never spends on an ordinary "should I" conversation unless the client asks for the council.
